# Y's Guys Hall of Fame Formula V1

**Status:** Locked foundation  
**Eligibility threshold:** 100 Hall Points

Reaching 100 Hall Points makes a player eligible for induction. It does not automatically induct the player.

## Rules

- Only official awards, career statistical milestones, and career wins contribute Hall Points.
- Milestone rewards are cumulative.
- Each milestone reward is earned once.
- Hall Points never decrease except to correct inaccurate league data.
- Attendance, records, popularity, founding status, Commissioner discretion, and community contributions do not add Hall Points.
- Milestones also unlock permanent profile banners.

## Hall Status Levels

| Hall Points | Status |
|---:|---|
| 0–24 | Career Beginning |
| 25–49 | Building a Résumé |
| 50–74 | League Standout |
| 75–99 | Hall Watch |
| 100+ | Hall of Fame Eligible |

An inducted player receives a permanent **Hall of Famer** status separate from the calculated point total.

## Planning Assumptions

The formula is calibrated around approximately 45 Sundays per year, 8–12 games per Sunday, games played to 15, and a target Hall eligibility timeline of roughly 2–4 years for deserving players.
